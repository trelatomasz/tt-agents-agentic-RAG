# Behavioral and product evidence

## First five days

Choose one internal compatibility-search journey, collect 30–50 real queries, baseline
search time and correctness, and deliver an evaluated walking skeleton. Reject external
users and purchasing actions. Continue at >=80% correctly cited queries, >=30% faster
median search and zero unsupported compatibility claims.

## Agent-driven development

Agents may read linked Jira/Confluence context, change an allowlisted repository, run
checks and open a pull request. They may not merge or deploy. Require least-privilege MCP
tools, audit logs, tests, human review and measured lead time, rework and escaped defects.

## Launch recommendation

Keep the assistant internal for two weeks. Require 95% citation correctness, agreed
compatibility accuracy, safe abstention, freshness and fallback. Product, engineering,
security and catalog ownership decide on **2026-08-26** whether to start a limited canary.

## GPC context

Known: the role supports GPC digital transformation through GCP, Vertex AI and full-
lifecycle delivery. Hypotheses: compatibility mistakes have commercial impact and supplier
identifiers/freshness vary. Ask: Which system owns fitment truth and supersessions? Which
catalog slices dominate search time and wrong-part returns?

## Temporary priority

Accuracy is the reversible constraint until the executive returns; measure speed and
adoption too. Continue evaluation and telemetry, preserve product decision rights and
review evidence on **2026-08-19**.

## Failed RAG story

A personal RAG system was built before validating its workflow. Running software had no
query set or acceptance threshold and remained unused. Record actual time/token cost before
using this story. The changed process requires a user, examples, baseline, cost ceiling and
stop date before infrastructure.
